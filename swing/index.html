A/B Test Power Analysis Calculator

This calculator helps determine the minimum detectable effect (MDE) for your A/B tests based on traffic, conversion rate, and experiment duration.

See Alexey's Swing Size Calculator for earlier version

Key features:

  • One-sided tests: Use when you only care about detecting improvements (recommended for most A/B tests)
  • 95% power recommended: Higher power means you're less likely to miss real effects
  • Both analysis methods: Shows results for both Frequentist (fixed sample) and Sequential (optional stopping) approaches
  • MDE is shown as relative lift: The percentage increase/decrease relative to your baseline conversion rate
  • Color coding: Green (<5% lift), Yellow (5-10% lift), Red (>10% lift)
  • Experiment recommendations: Get specific test design suggestions optimized for your traffic and conversion rate

Note: Sequential testing requires larger MDEs due to more conservative statistical thresholds to account for multiple looks at the data.
